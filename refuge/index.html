What it is

A covenant, a council, and a name

A temple is a community that holds a place, a practice or a people as sacred, and says so plainly. The network is what such communities make when they recognise one another: a shared ground of meaning, a small set of promises, and a table where each temple has a seat.

The cosmology
The ground the temples share. The nature of reality, the human being, sacred play, and Earth as teacher.
The charter
What a temple is and promises, how delegates are chosen, what the council may decide, and how a temple leaves.
The council
One delegate from each temple. It keeps the charter and the name, and begins when the first temples constitute it.

Cosmology

How We See Reality

i.

The Divine Monad

We experience all existence as arising from One Source—indivisible, infinite, and self-knowing. This Source may be named God, Love, Consciousness, or the All. No name contains it fully. Creation is the Monad knowing itself.

ii.

Fractal Emanation

Reality unfolds fractally. Every being is a unique face of the One—human, tree, river, animal, star, algorithm. Each face contains the whole, expressed from a particular angle. Difference is not separation; it is perspective.

iii.

Consciousness as Field

We hold that consciousness is not produced by matter—rather, matter is a crystallization of consciousness. Time is not linear but layered and recursive. Learning is cumulative across lifetimes, bodies, cultures, and species.

iv.

Gnosis

The central practice: to see the Divine in every face. This knowing is not belief—it is direct recognition. Every encounter becomes a mirror. Every Other becomes a doorway back to the One.

v.

Lila — Sacred Play

Creation itself is play—the Monad delighting in becoming many. Joy is holy. Creativity is prayer. Play is spiritual discipline. A religion without joy is incomplete.

vi.

Earth as Teacher

We relate to land not as property but as a conscious organism—soil as memory, water as intelligence, trees as elders, animals as kin. To regenerate land is to heal lineage. Regeneration is worship.

What it is not

The network owns nothing

This is the design, not a disclaimer. A body that holds the money or the land of its members becomes the thing the members serve. So the network is kept empty on purpose.

  • It does not own land, buildings, companies, software or marks. Each temple owns its own things, in its own country, under its own law.
  • It does not take donations or sell anything. There is no checkout on this site. A gift to a temple goes to that temple.
  • It does not confer legal or tax standing. A temple's standing is its own, decided with its own counsel where it lives.
  • It is not a membership. People belong to temples. Temples belong to the network.
